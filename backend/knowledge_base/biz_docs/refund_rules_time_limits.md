title: Refund Rules - Time Limits
tags: refund, time, conditions
policy_type: REFUND
content:
- Refund requests must be submitted within 7 days.
- Delivery failure refunds process within 3 days.
- Overcharge refunds require invoice verification.
- Partial refunds require evidence.
- Refunds follow original payment method.
- Record refund reasons for analysis.
exceptions:
- Regulatory complaints bypass time limits.
- Fraud checks may extend processing time.
- Bulk contracts have custom refund terms.
source: Internal Ops Policy v1
