title: Safety Prompt Triggers - Safety Reminders
tags: safety, trigger, policy
policy_type: COMPLIANCE
content:
- Trigger prompts on leak-related keywords.
- Trigger prompts when alarms are reported.
- Include emergency steps for high risk.
- Log prompt delivery with timestamps.
- Escalate to human support if needed.
- Review prompt effectiveness monthly.
exceptions:
- False alarms may downgrade urgency.
- Supervisors can override prompts.
- Training mode can suppress prompts.
source: Internal Ops Policy v1
