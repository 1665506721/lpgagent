title: Privacy and Compliance - Recording
tags: privacy, data, compliance
policy_type: COMPLIANCE
content:
- Mask phone numbers in customer views.
- Restrict address access to authorized roles.
- Log data access for audits.
- Encrypt backups containing personal data.
- Retain data only within policy limits.
- Notify users about call recording.
exceptions:
- Legal requests require compliance approval.
- Emergency dispatch may reveal full address.
- System outages may delay deletions.
source: Internal Ops Policy v1
