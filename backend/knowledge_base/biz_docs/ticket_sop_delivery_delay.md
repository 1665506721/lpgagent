title: Ticket SOP - Delivery Delay
tags: ticket, sop, complaint
policy_type: TICKET
content:
- Classify tickets by type and urgency.
- Contact customer within defined SLA.
- Log all actions and communication.
- Provide resolution steps and follow-up.
- Escalate repeated issues to supervisors.
- Close ticket after confirmation.
exceptions:
- Severe safety tickets require immediate escalation.
- VIP tickets follow priority handling.
- Anonymous tickets require minimum handling.
source: Internal Ops Policy v1
