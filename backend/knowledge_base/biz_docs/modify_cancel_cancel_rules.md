title: Order Modify and Cancel - Cancel Rules
tags: order, modify, cancel
policy_type: ORDER
content:
- Orders can be modified before dispatch only.
- Address changes require user confirmation.
- Cancellations before dispatch are fee-free.
- After dispatch, a cancellation fee may apply.
- Notify dispatch team after any change.
- Log reasons for modifications and cancellations.
exceptions:
- Supervisor can override in emergencies.
- VIP accounts may modify after dispatch.
- System errors allow free cancellation.
source: Internal Ops Policy v1
