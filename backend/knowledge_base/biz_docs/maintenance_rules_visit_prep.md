title: Maintenance Dispatch - Visit Prep
tags: maintenance, dispatch, sla
policy_type: MAINTENANCE
content:
- Safety issues require dispatch within 2 hours.
- Confirm customer availability before assigning.
- Provide arrival window to the customer.
- Prioritize leak reports over routine checks.
- Track dispatch time for SLA reporting.
- Follow up after service completion.
exceptions:
- Remote areas may extend SLA.
- Severe weather can delay visits.
- Customer reschedules reset SLA clock.
source: Internal Ops Policy v1
