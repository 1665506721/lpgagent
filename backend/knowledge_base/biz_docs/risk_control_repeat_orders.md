title: Risk Control - Repeat Orders
tags: risk, fraud, monitor
policy_type: COMPLIANCE
content:
- Flag repeated orders within short intervals.
- Verify phone number for high-value orders.
- Hold orders with mismatched addresses.
- Review payment failures before dispatch.
- Escalate suspicious activity to risk team.
- Log risk decisions for audits.
exceptions:
- Emergency orders can bypass some checks.
- Corporate accounts use separate thresholds.
- Manual review can release holds.
source: Internal Ops Policy v1
