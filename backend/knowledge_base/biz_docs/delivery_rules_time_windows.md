title: Delivery Rules - Time Windows
tags: delivery, schedule, coverage
policy_type: DELIVERY
content:
- Standard delivery window is 2 to 4 hours.
- Same-day delivery cut-off is 18:00.
- Service areas are reviewed quarterly.
- Notify customers of delays over 30 minutes.
- Allow one reschedule per order.
- Record promised vs actual delivery time.
exceptions:
- Holiday schedules follow published calendars.
- Remote zones may have longer windows.
- Severe weather can suspend delivery.
source: Internal Ops Policy v1
